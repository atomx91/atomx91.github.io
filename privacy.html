Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

YoutubeAnalytics ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube and TikTok channels.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos, manage playlists and update channel details on the owner's channel, and to read analytics for that channel.
  • TikTok: permission to publish videos to the owner's account and to read basic statistics of those videos.

How data is used

The app uses this access only to publish the owner's videos and to read their performance so the owner can improve future videos. It does not access other users' data.

Storage

Access tokens and analytics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions and in TikTok's app settings.
